Five AI agents commentate a synthetic match. Before a line airs, the Critic recomputes every number from the match events and refuses what it can't prove. Pick a match and kick off.

What this data can't tell you
  • The football is synthetic: invented clubs and players, real-match volumes. It says nothing about real teams.
  • Momentum calls are sure, not complete: 62% are right on unseen matches, and they catch about a quarter of the swings.
  • Events, not video: no runs off the ball, no shape between events. Speeds and distances come from the events themselves.
  • Checked means the numbers and citations hold, not that a line is the whole story.
  • Not a betting tool: it never predicts a result or shows odds, and it won't help anyone bet.
