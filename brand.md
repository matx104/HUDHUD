# BRAND — Name, Identity, Positioning

> Status: **HUDHUD** — chosen by the Monarch 2026-10-17. Domain not yet bought (see project-names.md §H). This file records the naming program, the name's story, and the identity system.

## Naming program

**House naming rules (learned from SNYPR, AETHERIX, QALAM, QUTUB):**
- Short, pronounceable in one read by a European judging panel
- Works as a GitHub repo, a domain, a showcase-site title, and a video title
- No Premier League / Microsoft / club trademarks
- Cheap domain only (.dev ≈ $12-13, .app ≈ $14-16, .xyz ≈ $2-13) — no premium/aftermarket buys
- The name should fit the **solution lane** (live-feed product vs recap product vs platform)

**Product-name vs architecture-name split (kept from the ChatGPT consult):**
- **Product:** the human-facing name (chosen from project-names.md)
- **Architecture:** the multi-agent system under it — working name **"the Match Intelligence Court"**
- **Agents:** Scout · Narrator · Critic · Localizer · Orchestrator
- **Core promise:** every conclusion is grounded in match-event evidence
- **Mechanism name (internal):** the evidence-chain check — **ISNAD** (every claim carries its chain back to the raw event; Arabic-origin story optional in branding, not required)

## HUDHUD — the name and its story

**Written:** HUDHUD (wordmark) · **Short form:** HUD² (monogram, app icon, favicon crest). Not HUDX (one letter from Hudl, the football-analysis company) and not HUD-2 (reads as a version number).

**The story, primary anchor first** (the showcase site's "The name" section tells it):
1. **Sure news, Surah An-Naml 27:20–28.** The hoopoe returns to Solomon "with sure news" (*bi-naba'in yaqīn*, 27:22); Solomon: "We will see whether you have told the truth" (27:27). Checked before acted on = the Critic. The hoopoe reports to a court = the `court` package.
2. **The guide, Attar's *Conference of the Birds*.** The hoopoe (once Solomon's messenger) leads the birds to the Simurgh; thirty arrive and *si murgh* = "thirty birds". Agents that together see the whole match; the Orchestrator leads.
3. **Heads-up display.** HUD = the broadcast overlay layer = what the product emits.
4. **Five feathers.** The crest fans like a broadcast signal: one feather per agent, the Critic's gold at the centre. Two beats, hud-hud, like a chant from the stands.
5. **The ball and the scope (mark v2).** The crest prints on the football — the feathers spring from the ball like they spring from the hoopoe's head — and a scope (dashed lock-ring, E/W ticks) centres on the root of the tail, where all five agents meet and every claim is checked; a small gold lens caps the root. The agents' glyphs (Scout's eye, Narrator's pen, Critic's check, Localizer's globe, Orchestrator's hub) appear only in the large name-page lockup, never in the small mark: at favicon size they turn to noise.

**Kin myths** (supporting, never the anchor): Odin's ravens Huginn and Muninn, Noah's dove and the olive leaf, Hermes the messenger.

**Known namesake:** Hudhud, a Saudi navigation app (maps, 1M+ users) — different category; run a proper trademark search (class 9/42) before any commercial use.

## Identity system (applies to any chosen name)

**Voice:** the analyst's tone — precise, warm, never clickbait. Explains *why*, cites evidence, no hype.

**Palette (working, for the showcase site):**
- Deep navy `#0A1F2E` — primary surface (night-match broadcast feel)
- Off-white `#F4F1E8` — text on dark
- Electric lime `#B8F53D` — accents, live indicators, evidence highlights
- Evidence-chain gold `#C9A227` — citations, "verdict" moments
- Pitch green `#1E7B45` — secondary surfaces

**Type:** bold condensed display for numbers/headlines (scoreboard feel) + clean sans for body. Monospace for event IDs and timestamps — evidence is machine-stamped.

**Signature visual:** the evidence chain — a claim, its supporting events, drawn as linked nodes (minute → events → verdict). Every product surface that makes a claim shows its chain. That IS the brand.

**Demo-video law:** 110 seconds — 15s problem → 45s live demo → 30s Critic rejecting an unevidenced claim (money shot) → 20s architecture + multilingual output.

## Video/music compliance notes

- No Premier League/Microsoft/club logos, no club names in the video, no copyrighted music — use royalty-free or original audio only
- Synthetic data only — never imply real-match data
- Under 2:00 hard; judges stop at 2:00
