The crest

A hoopoe's crest of five feathers, one for each agent: Scout, Narrator, Critic, Localizer and Orchestrator. The Critic's feather stands at the centre, in gold, because nothing airs until it has checked it.

The name

In Surah An-Naml, 27:20–28, the hoopoe returns to Solomon "with sure news", and Solomon checks it before acting on it. That check is the Critic.

Using it

On deep navy, the Critic's feather always gold. Never beside a league, club or broadcaster mark: every club and player in {{name}} is invented. The type is under the SIL Open Font License.
