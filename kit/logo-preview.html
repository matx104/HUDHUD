Every candidate keeps the concept and the palette: lime feathers, the Critic's gold at the centre and in the lens, chalk seams on the raised navy ball. Candidates 1 and 2 keep the current feather paths untouched; candidate 3 refines them (slimmer, tips held inside the ring). Pick a candidate (or mix: one scope with another lens, v3's feathers under the v1 scope) and it gets finished across every surface: the site's inline crests, the favicon, the studio slates, icons, og.png and the media kit.
